Locked to you, a clearer name, and the game keeps the focus:

- Every new connection now takes Face ID or your passcode, and pairing takes Windows Hello on your PC. Your phone needs pairing again once, and your PC needs a Windows PIN.

- The bar Windows shows while the game is shared now names `remote-play.3dx`.
- Starting in the tray no longer takes the focus away from the game.
- Chat from your phone now types into whichever chat box is active in the game, so tap the one you want first.
- A red dot on the tray icon shows when a phone is connected.
- Typing stops the moment the game leaves the front, even halfway through a message, and anything held down is let go.
- New on your phone: a full keyboard in three sizes, and a controller, on screen or paired over Bluetooth.

3DX Remote Play plays 3DXChat on your phone, from anywhere:

- Scan a code once, then open the page you saved, on Wi-Fi or mobile data. Nothing to install on the phone.
- Touch controls made for 3DX: tap to walk and click, drag to turn the camera, pinch to zoom, chat with your phone's keyboard.
- Only the game window and the game's sound are shared, encrypted, with the one phone you paired.
- Runs in the tray, and can open with Windows.

Windows may say the app is from an unknown publisher: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
